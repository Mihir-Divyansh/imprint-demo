Each voice below was cloned from 60 seconds of its recordings with F5-TTS, using all of the audio as prompts (multi-prompt guidance) and a light fine-tune, and then given a guide's delivery of the sentence (prosody transfer). Options used
  • Fine-tuning (gentle): only F5's time-conditioned layer-norm modulation, 50 steps.
  • Multi-prompt guidance: F5's prediction averaged over up to 5 prompts at every step, so all 60 s are used.
  • Guidance strength 1 (F5's default is 2).
  • Guided delivery: the guide's clear pitch accents (> 0.8 semitones), loudness (> 1.5 dB) and gently adjusted timing, moved onto the clone with Toucan's aligner and PSOLA.

Black, Carnegie Mellon University), used under its free licence, and the author's own laptop-microphone recordings (the author is also the guide in that run, which additionally uses input denoising). Models: F5-TTS v1 Base, ToucanTTS's aligner, Whisper large-v3-turbo, WavLM-Base-Plus-SV; all pretrained and open, run locally. These clones were made for a course project on few-shot voice cloning; do not use voice cloning to impersonate anyone.
